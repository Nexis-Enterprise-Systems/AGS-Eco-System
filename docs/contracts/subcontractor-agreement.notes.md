# Notes for the solicitor: sub-contractor agreement (painters and decorators)

This is AGS's existing sub-contractor agreement, loaded into the Eco System as a template. The 24 clauses are exactly as AGS currently uses them. Only the Schedule has changed: its values are now fields the system fills in for each sub-contractor (agreement number, date, name and address, commencement and completion dates, rate), and "Hourly Rate" is now "Rate", because the system can record a day, hourly or per-job rate. Nothing here has been reviewed by a lawyer. The points below are things I noticed on reading it; they are questions for you, not conclusions.

## Employment status and CIS (the main concern)
1. **Hourly rate with fixed pay days.** An hourly rate paid on set dates twice a month, with "pay statements", reads like wages. Please advise whether AGS should pay by price for the job, day rate or schedule of rates against applications, and whether the fixed pay dates help or hurt.
2. **Control.** Clause 8 says the sub-contractor uses their own initiative, but also must "comply with all reasonable and lawful requests of the Client". Clause 5 requires progress reports and compliance with Client procedures. Please check the balance, especially for individuals.
3. **Notice.** The Schedule says no notice is needed by either side, but clause 12 refers to "the required notice", and the Client may stop work at any time where Services are "unsatisfactory in the reasonable opinion of the Client". Please check these fit together.
4. **Restrictions (clause 6).** The undercutting and client-solicitation restrictions sit awkwardly beside clause 2 ("entitled to seek... contracts... to any third party") and the statement that the sub-contractor is in business on their own account. They also use "the Company", which is not defined (the document uses "the Client"). Please advise on enforceability and on the effect on status.
5. **Substitution (clause 2).** The right looks genuine on paper. In practice AGS's high-end clients may expect the named person to do the work. Please advise on how to keep the right real.
6. **Individuals and companies.** Clause 8 describes the parties as "independent companies", but many sub-contractors are individuals. Where a sub-contractor works through a limited company, the company, not the person, should be the party. The system currently names the individual and records which way they work; please say how the agreement should differ.
7. **CIS.** Please check clauses 9 and 10 and the pay statements meet CIS requirements, including timing and verification.

## Drafting points
8. **Undefined terms:** "Labour Only Services" (clauses 2 and 17), "Data Protection Legislation", "Personal Data", "Data Subject", and "the Company". The "Substitute" definition appears inside clause 2 rather than in clause 1.
9. **Clause 2, last paragraph** says the Schedule specifies "the fee payable... and such disbursements"; the Schedule has a rate only.
10. **Clause 18.** It makes the sub-contractor the data processor of AGS's data, which looks the wrong way round for this relationship, and refers to the European Economic Area rather than UK data protection law.
11. **Clause 17.** The Client is liable for "proper and reasonable loss, damage or injury to the subcontractor" with no limit, and says AGS will insure "Labour Only subcontractors". Please check this matches AGS's actual insurance.
12. **Term.** The expected completion date is the commencement date plus 365 days. The system does not yet send annual reconfirmation reminders; that is planned.
13. **Electronic signature.** The person signs by typing their full name, and the system keeps the signed wording, their name and the time. Please confirm this is acceptable for this agreement.

## How the system uses it
The Schedule fields fill in when the person reaches the agreement step: the agreement number is allocated when AGS creates the invite (format AGS-SC-0001, counting up), the agreement date is the day the invite was created, and the commencement date is the day of signing. The signed copy is stored permanently with the exact wording, and a later change to the template does not alter anything already signed. Mark any changes in the Word copy and the template can be updated as a new version.
