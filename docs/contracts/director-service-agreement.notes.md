# Notes for the solicitor: Managing Director service agreement

Prepared by Claude for AGS Decorating & Refurbishment Services as a first draft. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer. The points below are the ones I could not settle, or that depend on facts about the company.

## Facts to confirm
1. Exact company name, number and registered office. Who are the directors and shareholders? If the MD is the only director and only shareholder, how should the agreement be approved and recorded?
2. Is the MD an employee, or an office-holder only? The draft makes the MD an employee as well as a director, since payroll is run through PAYE internally.
3. Salary, pay frequency, hours, place of work, holiday, notice, pension rate. The values are fields in the system and are left blank until agreed.
4. Whether the MD is the Company's only worker. This affects pension auto-enrolment duties, and so clause 7.5.

## Points of law to check
- **Approval and conflict.** The MD signs on both sides unless another director signs. Check sections 177 (declaring an interest in a proposed transaction) and 231 (contracts with a sole member who is also a director, which must be in writing or recorded in a written memorandum) of the Companies Act 2006, and the articles.
- **Section 188, Companies Act 2006.** A guaranteed term of more than two years needs members' approval. The draft has no fixed term, with a notice period set in the fields. Please check that the notice period does not amount to one.
- **Section 228.** Directors' service contracts must be kept available for inspection by members. Clause 21.2 says so.
- **Written statement of particulars.** The draft is intended to be the section 1 statement. Employment Rights Act changes since 2025 may add mandatory content or change qualifying periods. Please check against the current law.
- **Restrictive covenants (clause 14).** Check enforceability and length. The Government has consulted on limiting non-compete clauses, so please say if any change affects the draft. The field {{restricted_period_months}} is left open on purpose.
- **Working Time Regulations (clause 6.2).** Confirm whether the unmeasured working time exception applies to this role, and whether the opt-out is needed.
- **Pension (clause 7.5).** Confirm whether auto-enrolment duties apply when the MD is the only worker.
- **Termination payments (clause 7.6, 13).** Check the treatment of payments for loss of office, PILON and any restriction on payments to directors, and the tax treatment.
- **Indemnity and insurance (clause 17).** Check against sections 232 to 238.
- **Intellectual property (clause 12).** Check this is adequate for design work and documents produced for clients.
- **Anything missing.** Share option or bonus terms, car or vehicle, private medical, death in service, a disciplinary procedure, a long-term sickness position, and post-termination cooperation. None are in the draft.

## How the system uses the document
The agreement is a template with fields in double braces, for example {{director_full_name}}. When the MD's record is created, the system fills the fields, saves a frozen copy of the wording and the values, and records who accepted it and when. A later edit to the template does not change a signed agreement. Any change to wording is made in Settings, People and HR, Contract templates, and moves the template to a new version. Please mark any changes to wording in the draft and I will update the template.
